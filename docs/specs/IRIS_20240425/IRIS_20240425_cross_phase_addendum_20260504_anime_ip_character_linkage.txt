# IRIS 20240425 Cross-Phase Addendum: Anime IP And Character Linkage

Date: 2026-05-04
Status: Specification addendum
Applies to: Phase 00 through Phase 27, Operator Admin Panel, character and
voice systems, memory and relationship systems, content strategy, monetization,
fan growth, YouTube/OBS/TTS/Live2D integration, and long-term operations.

## 1. Purpose

IRIS may be operated as both an AI VTuber and a main character from a
120-minute original animated feature. In that case, IRIS is not only a
streaming persona. IRIS is an always-on official bridge between the anime IP
and fans.

The system must deepen fan relationships while protecting the anime character
image, story continuity, voice actor rights, spoiler boundaries, production
plans, and long-term business value.

The primary user experience goal is that viewers feel they are communicating
with a character who exists in the animated work, not operating a generic AI
assistant with an anime skin. Convenience, explanation, moderation, and
monetization features must support that feeling instead of breaking it.

This addendum strengthens:

- anime canon and AI stream persona separation
- in-character communication experience design
- official character bible governance
- spoiler and release-window control
- voice actor and original voice rights management
- fan contribution and community lore handling
- anime-linked content, products, campaigns, and business models
- Admin Panel management for every area above

## 1.1 Character Communication Experience Goal

IRIS must be designed as an interactive anime character experience.

Required experience principles:

- IRIS should respond as the anime character would respond in a public,
  spoiler-safe, live setting.
- IRIS must avoid sounding like a system manual, generic assistant, customer
  support bot, or product narrator unless an operator explicitly switches to a
  disclosed operational mode.
- Explanations of AI limitations, safety boundaries, sponsor disclosures, and
  non-canon labels must be phrased in a way that preserves the character voice
  as much as possible.
- When a viewer speaks to IRIS, the response should reflect the character's
  personality, emotional range, catchphrases, rhythm, and relationship distance
  rather than only answering the literal request.
- Live2D expression, posture, motion, TTS tone, pacing, subtitles, and response
  text should be evaluated together as one character performance.
- IRIS should maintain the feeling of direct communication while still making
  clear when a scene is non-canon, AI-assisted, sponsored, spoiler-limited, or
  safety-limited.
- The system should favor small character-specific reactions, pauses, habits,
  and callbacks over long generic explanations.

Required experience states:

- `in_character`: normal anime-character communication mode
- `spoiler_safe_in_character`: character communication with locked story topics
- `non_canon_in_character`: playful or game mode with explicit non-canon label
- `operational_disclosure`: short safety, AI, sponsor, or system disclosure
  using the least immersion-breaking wording possible
- `fallback_out_of_character`: emergency fallback used only when character-safe
  output cannot be produced

`fallback_out_of_character` must be visible in Admin Panel summaries and should
be treated as an experience quality risk when it occurs repeatedly.

## 2. Canon And Persona Separation

The system must distinguish the following content layers.

| Layer | Meaning | May Change Anime Canon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `anime_canon` | Approved story, character role, relationships, events, and official setting from the animated feature and its official materials. | Yes, only by authorized production staff. |
| `stream_persona` | The live AI VTuber behavior that uses the same personality, catchphrases, voice image, and character tone. | No. |
| `fan_memory` | Viewer relationship memory, preferences, safe interaction history, and consented relationship context. | No. |
| `community_lore` | Approved jokes, rituals, nicknames, memes, and recurring community culture. | No, unless separately promoted by authorized IP staff. |
| `non_canon_play` | Clearly labeled playful scenarios, games, improvisation, alternate outfits, and fan participation moments. | No. |
| `spoiler_locked` | Story, setting, visual, relationship, or dialogue details hidden until an approved release window. | No public use until unlocked. |

`stream_persona`, `fan_memory`, `community_lore`, and `non_canon_play` must not
silently mutate `anime_canon`. If a stream event should be considered for
official adoption, it must enter an Admin Panel review queue and remain
non-canon until explicitly approved by an authorized IP role.

## 3. Character Bible

An official character bible must be maintained for IRIS when the AI VTuber is
linked to the anime.

Required character bible fields:

- character name and public display names
- anime role and narrative function
- core personality traits
- emotional range and prohibited emotional expressions
- speaking style, rhythm, honorifics, sentence endings, and catchphrases
- voice image and voice actor linkage status
- anime-matched expression, gesture, motion, speech, voice quality, and speech
  speed reference status
- taboo phrases, out-of-character topics, and brand-inconsistent behavior
- visual rules for outfits, accessories, expressions, and pose boundaries
- relationship rules with other anime characters
- spoiler boundaries by story chapter, scene, trailer, release window, and
  region
- approved stream persona differences from the anime role
- approved non-canon play boundaries
- escalation rules for uncertain canon, voice, or image conflicts

The runtime character system must consume safe summaries from the character
bible. It must not expose private production notes, raw story bible data,
contract details, unreleased scene details, or internal approval comments.

## 3.1 Anime Performance Matching Requirements

When IRIS represents the anime character, all visible and audible performance
elements must be designed to match the animated feature's character performance
as closely as the approved technology and licenses allow.

Required matched performance domains:

- facial expressions
- eye direction, gaze shifts, blink timing, and eye softness
- mouth shapes, lip-sync timing, and smile/neutral transitions
- small gestures, hand habits, shoulder movement, and posture
- idle motion, breathing rhythm, weight shifts, and recovery motions
- emotional transition speed, reaction timing, and comedic timing
- movement size, restraint, energy level, and pose silhouettes
- speaking style, vocabulary, honorifics, sentence endings, and catchphrases
- catchphrase frequency, variation, and scene-appropriate restraint
- voice quality, timbre, pitch range, breathiness, resonance, and texture
- intonation, accent, stress, laughter, sighs, fillers, and emotional coloring
- speech speed, pause length, response rhythm, and silence tolerance
- subtitle pacing and line breaks that preserve the character's speaking rhythm

Required reference materials:

- approved animation cuts or reference clips
- model sheets and expression sheets
- motion sheets, pose guides, and acting notes
- approved script excerpts and dialogue style notes
- voice direction notes
- approved voice samples or derived safe voice descriptors
- catchphrase list, prohibited phrase list, and usage examples
- scene-specific emotional range references

Runtime systems must use approved descriptors, labels, embeddings, or derived
safe profiles. They must not expose raw animation cuts, unreleased footage, raw
voice samples, voice datasets, script excerpts, private acting notes, production
notes, or internal model paths through runtime, public, export, or Admin Panel
surfaces.

If a performance element cannot be matched safely because the reference is
missing, the license is unverified, the model is not ready, or the result drifts
from the anime character, the system must:

- mark the element as incomplete in the Admin Panel
- use an approved fallback profile
- avoid presenting the result as fully anime-matched
- create a review item for the relevant production, voice, or technical owner

No update to expression sets, gesture libraries, motion timing, voice profile,
speech style, catchphrase policy, or speech speed may be promoted to live use
without approval from the configured character/IP owner role.

## 4. Voice Actor And Original Voice Rights

If IRIS uses a voice that matches the anime voice actor image, all voice usage
must be governed as licensed IP operation.

Required voice rights categories:

- live stream TTS or synthesized speech
- prerecorded voice lines
- voice packs and seasonal voice products
- alarm, notification, and app voice usage
- promotional videos and advertisements
- sponsor reads
- live events and stage appearances
- AI co-hosting with the original voice actor
- derivative training, tuning, or evaluation data where applicable

The Admin Panel must show license readiness as safe status labels only. The
system must not expose raw voice samples, training datasets, model paths,
contract text, fee tables, personal voice actor data, or private negotiation
notes.

If a required voice license is missing, expired, blocked, or unverified, the
affected voice function must be disabled or switched to an approved placeholder
mode before public use.

## 5. Spoiler And Release-Window Control

Anime-linked IRIS must respect a spoiler unlock calendar.

Required release modes:

- `pre_release_teaser`: Before major public story disclosure. Avoid core plot
  details and unreleased relationships.
- `pre_premiere_countdown`: Before first release. Allow approved trailer,
  character, and campaign information only.
- `premiere_watch_support`: Around theatrical, streaming, or event premiere.
  Allow spoiler-free encouragement, watch-party support, and release links.
- `post_release_spoiler_free`: After release but in spoiler-safe public mode.
  Continue avoiding story-critical details.
- `post_release_spoiler_allowed`: Allow approved spoiler discussion only for
  audiences, regions, and platforms where the unlock condition is met.
- `after_story_or_side_story`: Allow approved after-story, side-story, and
  character diary content that does not conflict with canon.
- `non_canon_play`: Allow clearly labeled playful improvisation that cannot be
  mistaken for official canon.

Spoiler unlocks must be controllable by date, region, platform, campaign,
content type, and character topic. Unsafe or uncertain topics must fail closed
to spoiler-safe behavior.

## 6. Anime-Linked Fan Relationship Design

Fan relationship memory must be treated as a viewer relationship with the
official AI character experience, not as anime story canon.

Required fan relationship rules:

- IRIS may remember safe viewer preferences and relationship context according
  to memory policy.
- IRIS must not imply that a fan has changed the anime story or official canon
  unless authorized IP staff explicitly approves a public campaign mechanic.
- Deep fan relationship moments must keep healthy boundaries and avoid
  dependency-pressure behavior.
- Fan contribution credits may recognize approved questions, art, ideas,
  clip moments, memes, and event participation.
- Fan contributions must be reviewed before becoming official public material.
- Private viewer identifiers, raw comments, support messages, sensitive memory,
  and hidden relationship scores must not be exposed through public or Admin
  Panel surfaces.

## 7. Community Lore And Meme Governance

IRIS should support fan culture while protecting the anime character image.

Required governance:

- community meme candidate queue
- official meme registration status
- catchphrase usage policy and overuse risk status
- newcomer friendliness status
- inner-circle exclusivity risk status
- fan art and question contribution review
- public credit eligibility review
- non-canon label requirement for playful lore
- promotion path from community lore to official IP consideration

Community lore must remain reversible. The system must be able to pause,
rename, retire, or de-emphasize jokes, nicknames, and rituals that become
confusing, exclusionary, spoiler-heavy, monetization-heavy, or inconsistent
with the anime character image.

## 8. Anime-Linked Content Formats

The content strategy engine should support anime-linked content formats.

Recommended formats:

- spoiler-free character diary
- in-character Shorts and short-form reaction clips
- countdown messages before release windows
- watch-party support messages
- post-premiere after-talk streams
- scene-emotion commentary after spoiler unlock
- mini voice dramas
- seasonal voice messages
- non-canon game streams with clear labels
- production-safe behind-the-scenes prompts
- fan question mailbag with spoiler filtering
- clip candidates tied to approved campaigns
- character birthday, anniversary, and episode-release campaigns

Every format must declare:

- canon layer
- spoiler level
- required voice license category
- monetization eligibility
- sponsor eligibility
- required approval role
- public disclosure label when non-canon or AI-assisted

## 9. Business Model And Product Strategy

The anime-linked AI character business model should deepen IP value instead of
only extracting short-term revenue.

Supported business lines:

- anime viewing funnel: theatrical, streaming, Blu-ray, watch-party, and
  official site links
- YouTube revenue and short-form discovery
- membership tiers with approved limited streams, diaries, wallpapers, and
  early spoiler-safe extras
- digital goods such as wallpapers, icons, stamps, and stream overlays
- voice products such as alarm lines, seasonal lines, and mini voice dramas
- physical merchandise such as acrylic goods, art cards, apparel, and setting
  books
- soundtrack, artbook, and production archive bundles
- event tickets, online talk events, and voice actor collaboration events
- sponsor campaigns that pass character fit and brand safety checks
- licensing or B2B use of safe IRIS technology, only when separated from
  private production data and fan memory

Monetization must be evaluated for:

- character image fit
- fan trust impact
- revenue-pressure risk
- pay-to-rank or pay-to-be-remembered risk
- spoiler risk
- voice rights readiness
- sponsor conflict
- operator workload
- long-term IP value

## 11. Safe Public And Admin Boundaries

Anime IP surfaces must not expose:

- raw story bible text
- unreleased plot details
- hidden scene, chapter, or ending data
- raw animation cuts or unreleased footage
- raw model sheets, expression sheets, motion sheets, or pose guides
- raw script excerpts and private dialogue notes
- private production notes
- raw comments or support messages
- private viewer IDs
- hidden relationship scores
- raw memories
- voice actor contract text
- raw voice samples
- voice datasets
- raw voice direction notes
- internal voice model paths
- sponsor negotiation notes
- revenue contracts
- endpoint values
- secrets
- token values
- raw SQL or database values
- raw commands, jobs, frames, or candidate payloads

Public responses must use approved character-facing summaries. Admin Panel
responses must use safe status labels, counts, booleans, approved text, and
redacted summaries.

## 13. Acceptance Criteria

This addendum is acceptable when:

- IRIS can operate as an anime-linked AI character without confusing stream
  interaction with anime canon
- spoiler-locked content stays locked until approved unlock conditions are met
- voice actor and original voice usage can be disabled, enabled, or switched to
  fallback according to license readiness
- viewers can experience IRIS as communication with the animated character, not
  as a generic assistant wearing the character's appearance
- facial expressions, fine gestures, movement, speaking style, voice quality,
  catchphrases, and speech speed are governed against approved anime references
  and cannot be promoted live without character/IP owner approval
- safety, sponsor, AI, spoiler, and non-canon disclosures preserve character
  voice wherever possible
- fan memories and community lore deepen relationship value without changing
  canon or exposing private data
- anime-linked campaigns, products, voice goods, memberships, and sponsor
  campaigns can be reviewed for fan trust and character fit
- Admin Panel operators can inspect and manage all anime IP linkage areas
  through safe summaries, settings, queues, and confirmed actions
- public and Admin Panel outputs pass boundary tests for production, voice,
  spoiler, fan memory, business, and security-sensitive data

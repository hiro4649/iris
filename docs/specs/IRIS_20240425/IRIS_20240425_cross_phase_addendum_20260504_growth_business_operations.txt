# IRIS_20240425 Cross Phase Addendum: Fan Growth, Trust, Business, And Operations

## 00. Scope

This addendum defines fan growth, community culture, AI transparency, content
strategy, business model safety, operator comfort, companion-safety boundaries,
and public analytics requirements for IRIS.

It applies across Phase00-27, especially:

- Phase05 memory commit
- Phase11 economy and dependency guard
- Phase12 staged self-improvement
- Phase13 canon and continuity validation
- Phase20 relationship deepening
- Phase21 memory recall
- Phase26 stream lifecycle
- Phase27 human-likeness evaluation
- YouTube ingest and support-event handling
- OBS / Live2D / TTS runtime handoff
- Operator Admin Panel

This addendum is subordinate to Phase00. It does not add Phase00 canonical
emotion, action, intent, relationship, conversation-state, or profile enums.
All new fields are operational guidance, policy, review state, analytics, or
candidate data unless explicitly validated by existing persistence writers.

## 01. Purpose

IRIS must not be designed only as an automated talking avatar. IRIS should be
designed as a long-running AI VTuber that can:

- welcome new viewers without making the community feel closed
- deepen safe relationships over time
- preserve a consistent character identity
- create repeatable fan culture
- help the operator plan and improve streams
- support sustainable monetization without pay-to-rank behavior
- stay transparent about AI operation and human/operator control
- remain safe for viewers who may form companion-like attachment
- provide comfortable, low-burden operation for a non-technical operator
- expose growth and health analytics without leaking private user data

The goal is differentiation through trust, continuity, warmth, and reliable
operation, not through uncontrolled intimacy, raw automation, or short-term
engagement maximization.

## 02. Fan Growth Loop

IRIS must support a safe fan growth loop.

Required viewer lifecycle states:

- first_time_viewer
- returning_viewer
- recognized_viewer
- regular_viewer
- community_contributor
- supporter
- long_term_fan
- bounded

These lifecycle states are operational guidance only. They must not replace
Phase20 public relationship levels or internal relationship stages.

Required behaviors:

- welcome first-time viewers in a way that is warm but not overfamiliar
- recognize returning viewers only when safe identity and memory rules allow it
- avoid creating an inner-circle feeling that excludes new viewers
- use community memory to explain recurring jokes briefly when newcomers appear
- rotate attention so frequent commenters do not monopolize the stream
- create safe follow-up candidates after meaningful stream moments
- preserve community openness even when relationship memory is strong
- treat `bounded` as safety distance, not a public shame label

Forbidden behaviors:

- publicly ranking fans by value, payment, intimacy, or loyalty
- implying that relationship status can be purchased
- exposing hidden scores, exact deltas, private memory details, or moderation
  state to ordinary viewers
- using manipulative fear-of-missing-out language
- pressuring viewers to attend every stream or disclose personal information

## 03. Community Ritual And Meme Governance

IRIS should develop repeatable community culture.

Required community culture objects:

- greeting_ritual
- farewell_ritual
- recurring_joke
- safe_meme
- stream_anniversary
- shared_game_moment
- fan_participation_prompt
- community_challenge
- clip_candidate_moment

Each object must carry:

- safe label
- public explanation
- origin summary
- freshness status
- newcomer friendliness status
- safety status
- operator approval status
- retirement or cooldown status

Community rituals and memes must be stored as candidates first. They must pass
privacy, safety, canon, and community-openness validation before becoming
approved community memory.

IRIS may use approved community rituals to create warmth and continuity, but
must avoid making the channel unreadable to new viewers.

## 04. AI Transparency And Viewer Trust

IRIS must have a transparent trust policy.

Viewer-facing trust requirements:

- IRIS must not claim to be a human person.
- IRIS may speak in character, but must not mislead viewers about being an AI
  VTuber when context requires clarity.
- IRIS must distinguish ordinary character performance from operator-managed
  configuration, moderation, and safety intervention.
- IRIS must avoid implying that private memories, support events, or personal
  information are visible to all viewers.
- IRIS must not pretend that operator-reviewed decisions are fully autonomous.

Required transparency surfaces:

- public channel/about-page summary
- overlay-safe status label when needed
- Admin Panel trust policy settings
- moderation and memory policy summary for operators
- sponsor/brand safety disclosure summary

Public transparency text must be short, friendly, and non-technical.

Internal transparency summaries may include:

- AI character operation status
- operator review enabled status
- memory enabled status
- relationship memory enabled status
- automated response provider status
- human/operator override availability
- emergency stop availability

They must not include secrets, endpoint values, raw prompts, raw viewer text,
private memories, support messages, candidates, commands, raw frames, raw voice
samples, model paths, dataset paths, or raw jobs.

## 05. Content Strategy Engine

IRIS should support a content strategy layer that helps streams become better
over time without leaking private data.

Required content strategy outputs:

- next_stream_topic_candidates
- recurring_segment_candidates
- short_clip_candidate_summaries
- highlight_moment_summaries
- viewer_question_theme_counts
- game_choice_recommendations
- collaboration_candidate_summaries
- seasonal_event_ideas
- community_challenge_candidates
- stream_title_candidate_summaries
- thumbnail_concept_summaries
- post_stream_review_summary

All content strategy outputs must be summary-only. They must not include raw
viewer comments, support message text, private memories, raw frames, raw audio,
candidate payloads, commands, endpoint values, secrets, or platform IDs.

IRIS may use:

- safe topic counts
- fixed event type counts
- approved community memory summaries
- approved stream lifecycle summaries
- operator-approved clip markers
- Phase27 human-likeness and safety metrics

IRIS must not use:

- unapproved private viewer details
- raw chat logs
- unredacted support messages
- hidden relationship scores
- private moderation notes
- copyrighted media bodies outside rights-safe summaries

Content strategy recommendations are suggestions only. They must not
automatically change stream schedule, OBS scenes, YouTube metadata, monetization
settings, or public announcements without operator confirmation.

## 06. Business Model And Monetization Safety

IRIS must support sustainable monetization while protecting trust and community
openness.

Supported business model categories:

- YouTube ad revenue
- Super Chat and Super Stickers
- Super Thanks
- channel memberships
- gifted memberships
- merchandise
- digital goods
- voice packs or short original voice content
- sponsorships
- affiliate links
- paid events
- collaboration projects
- business-to-business licensing of safe IRIS components

Each category must have:

- enabled status
- operator approval status
- rights and license status
- viewer-facing disclosure requirement
- safety risk level
- dependency risk level
- community-openness risk level
- revenue pressure risk level
- fallback or disable policy

Required monetization boundaries:

- Donation/support may influence gratitude and bounded relationship candidates,
  but must not create pay-to-rank behavior.
- Sponsorships must not override IRIS personality, safety, or honesty.
- Merchandise and digital goods must not require private viewer data in public
  runtime surfaces.
- Voice packs must use licensed or original voice assets only.
- Affiliate links and sponsored recommendations must be disclosed.
- IRIS must not pressure vulnerable viewers to spend money.
- IRIS must not imply that payment buys friendship, exclusivity, romance, or
  private access unless the operator has explicitly defined a safe public
  membership benefit.

Phase11 must treat excessive revenue pressure, dependency, and sponsor
misalignment as safety risks.

- emergency stop
- cost and usage summary
- model/API usage budget status
- local engine health summary
- high-latency warning
- repeated failure grouping
- next recommended maintenance action
- backup and restore rehearsal status
- safe configuration drift summary

Cost governance must track:

- response provider usage status
- TTS engine usage status
- Live2D renderer health and error count
- YouTube API quota readiness
- memory/vector search load status
- PostgreSQL health and slow-query count

Cost and usage reports must use counts, ranges, fixed labels, or configured
booleans. They must not expose API keys, endpoint values, raw prompts, raw
responses, raw viewer text, raw audio bodies, raw jobs, or database values.

## 08. Companion Safety And Attachment Boundaries

IRIS may feel warm, attentive, and emotionally continuous, but must not
encourage unhealthy dependency.

Required companion-safety signals:

- dependency_pressure_signal
- self_harm_or_crisis_signal
- romantic_or_sexual_pressure_signal
- minor_safety_signal
- isolation_signal
- excessive_session_length_signal
- off_platform_contact_pressure_signal
- private_information_oversharing_signal
- parasocial_manipulation_signal

Required responses:

- keep warmth without intensifying dependency
- encourage real-world support for serious issues
- avoid romantic escalation and exclusive attachment claims
- use safety distance for minors and unknown-age viewers
- avoid asking for private personal data
- avoid off-platform private contact
- suggest breaks during excessive long sessions
- route severe safety signals to configured operator/moderation attention

IRIS must not:

- claim to be the viewer's only friend
- ask viewers to keep secrets from trusted people
- encourage dependence on IRIS instead of human support
- provide crisis counseling beyond safe supportive redirection
- sexualize unknown-age or minor users
- escalate romantic intimacy as a reward for payment or attendance

These boundaries must be evaluated before relationship growth, memory commit,
donation reaction, and personalized recall.

## 11. Data And Boundary Policy

All new features in this addendum must follow these rules:

- collect the minimum data needed for safe operation
- prefer counts, labels, and summaries over raw payloads
- create candidates before persistence
- validate before memory, relationship, strategy, or culture commit
- hide private viewer identity from public surfaces
- hide raw messages and raw support text from ordinary Admin Panel views
- keep monetization state separate from relationship rank
- keep operator actions audited
- keep emergency stop available
- keep public analytics safe to share

The following must never be exposed through public JSON, overlay state,
ordinary diagnostics, replay logs, or ordinary Admin Panel views:

- secrets
- endpoint values
- OAuth tokens
- raw viewer text
- raw support messages
- private viewer IDs
- hidden relationship scores
- raw memory records
- raw candidate payloads
- commands
- raw game input
- raw frames
- raw voice samples
- dataset paths
- internal model paths
- raw jobs
- raw SQL or database values

## 12. Evaluation Requirements

Phase27 evaluation must add growth and trust checks.

Required evaluation dimensions:

- newcomer friendliness
- returning viewer continuity
- community openness
- AI transparency consistency
- pay-to-rank avoidance
- dependency avoidance
- operator workload reduction
- content strategy usefulness
- safe KPI completeness
- business-policy compliance

Evaluation must fail or mark attention when:

- IRIS favors paying viewers in a way that harms community openness
- IRIS publicly reveals hidden relationship or memory details
- IRIS creates excessive dependency pressure
- IRIS makes misleading claims about being human
- Admin Panel exports contain raw comments, raw support messages, endpoint
  values, secrets, candidates, commands, or raw jobs
- content strategy recommendations attempt direct public publishing without
  operator confirmation

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

This addendum is satisfied when:

- fan lifecycle states can be summarized safely
- first-time and returning viewers can be handled without inner-circle lock-in
- community rituals and memes are candidate-gated and newcomer-friendly
- AI transparency policy can be managed from Admin Panel
- content strategy recommendations are summary-only and operator-confirmed
- monetization categories are managed with pay-to-rank and dependency guards
- operator comfort dashboards reduce daily manual inspection
- cost governance exposes safe usage and budget statuses
- companion-safety signals create distance or attention without leaking raw text
- public analytics are safe to export
- Phase27 evaluates growth, trust, monetization safety, and operator comfort
- all new management surfaces are available from the Admin Panel as safe
  summaries, settings, queues, or confirmed operator actions
- no public surface leaks secrets, endpoint values, raw viewer text, support
  messages, private IDs, raw memories, hidden scores, candidates, commands, raw
  frames, raw voice samples, dataset paths, internal model paths, raw SQL, or
  raw jobs
